You can have skunk.club fetch each CS2 match you finish and keep it on your profile with a 3D replay, so you never have to upload a demo by hand. Steam has to hand over two things first, once.

Set it up

  1. Open the tracking page and press Sign in through Steam.
  2. Open Steam Support: CS2, Access to Your Match History.
  3. Press Create Authentication Code. Copy the code, then copy Your most recently completed match token from the same page.
  4. Paste both into the form on the tracking page and press Start tracking.

The form has one optional field, an older match token. Give it a share code from any match in the last 30 days (in CS2's match list, use Copy share link) and the site fetches everything played since. Valve deletes replays after 30 days, so for older matches you can only upload the demos CS2 downloaded.

What happens next

The site checks for new matches every 15 minutes. A match you finish from now on shows up on your profile after the next check, each with its replay.

What is kept, and how to stop

Your authentication code and match token are kept only to fetch your matches from Steam. They are deleted when tracking stops. You can stop on the tracking page with Stop tracking, or revoke the code on the same Steam support page. Profiles and matches are public. To have a profile hidden, write to the address on the contact page.
