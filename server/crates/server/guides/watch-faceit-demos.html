You can open a FACEIT match as a 3D replay. Whether it opens straight away depends on whether the site can get the demo.

Paste the match room link

  1. Open the match on FACEIT and copy the link of its match room.
  2. Paste it into the box on the home page. The box labels it a match, and the button reads Watch replay.
  3. If the site can get the demo, the replay opens. If it cannot, the site tells you that FACEIT demos must be uploaded.

Upload the demo yourself

The match room has a demo download. Download the demo there, then upload it on the home page: press Upload a demo or drop the file on the page. The upload takes .dem, .dem.bz2 and .dem.gz files.

Track your FACEIT matches

If you have set up tracking, your profile has an On FACEIT block listing your recent FACEIT matches with their map, score and result. A match the site holds has a Replay link. For the rest there is Watch on FACEIT, which opens the match room, and, where an upload can be attached to the row, Upload the demo, which opens the upload page. A demo you upload is matched to the row by its map, score and players. FACEIT matches also have their own FACEIT tab among the match modes on a profile.

FACEIT's own data is labelled as FACEIT's and is not counted with the rest of your stats. skunk.club is not affiliated with FACEIT.

To try it, paste a match room link.
