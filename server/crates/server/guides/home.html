What it does

Drop a .dem file on the page, paste a CS2 share code, or paste a FACEIT match room link, and the match opens as a 3D replay in your browser: every round, any player's view, the kills as they happen. There is nothing to install.

Every match has a page with each player's stats, and every player has a profile with their rating, K/D, ADR and per-map numbers across the matches the site holds. Pro tournament demos are added as they become available.
